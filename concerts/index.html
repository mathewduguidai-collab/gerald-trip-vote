BEHIND THE SCORES

More than a play count.

Each artist is ranked against 3,715 distinct Spotify album artists. Open a concert to see lifetime plays, lifetime hours, last-12-month plays, and last-12-month hours—each with its rank. Whole-show scores reward multiple artists you listen to, especially explicit favorites.

Scores are preference estimates, not ticket availability. Most events are regional discovery candidates; verify dates, bills, ticket inventory and actual driving routes on official pages before making plans. A missing ticket status means unknown, not available. This public snapshot does not update automatically.
