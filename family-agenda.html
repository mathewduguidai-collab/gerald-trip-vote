Mon19

West Baden & French Lick

Historic dome · gardens · optional slots and bowling

How Monday works

10:30 AM: Leave the cabin for West Baden and French Lick. Explore the huge historic dome and gardens, enjoy lunch, and wander the resort.

Split up if wanted: Interested adults can visit French Lick Casino. Others can browse resort shops, try tile painting or bowling, or simply relax. Keep the walking short and include seated breaks. The resort's ADA-accessible shuttle connects its hotels, so nobody has to walk the entire complex. We won't plan the longer, less-accessible behind-the-scenes tour. Save the bigger antique-shopping trip for Thursday.

Tue20

Fossils → visit Colleen → dinner

Falls of the Ohio first · Pumpy & Pepper · dinner together

Family visit
View Tuesday's plans

The plan: An easy-paced stop at Falls of the Ohio, then arrive at Colleen's apartment around 3:30 PM after her 1:00–2:20 PM class. Time with her, Pumpy and Pepper, and dinner together.

10:30 AM ET: Leave the cabin (nobody needs to get up early).

~12:15–1:30 PM: Falls of the Ohio Interpretive Center in Clarksville, just across from Louisville. See the 390-million-year-old fossils, river overlook and indoor exhibits. Tuesday museum hours: 9 AM–5 PM. The indoor center is accessible, has a seated 14-minute film, and has a wheelchair available if helpful.

For anyone who wants to venture onto the outdoor fossil beds, that can be a separate option if the river level allows. The natural rock surface is uneven, so there's no expectation that everyone walks out there.

~1:30–2:30 PM: Relaxed sit-down lunch in southern Indiana. One nearby option: Orange Clover Kitchen & More (listed 4.5 stars, open Tuesday through 2:30 PM). If we're running late, pick somewhere open longer instead.

~2:30–3:30 PM: Travel to Colleen's place, with some time built in for traffic and parking.

3:30 PM: Visit Colleen at her apartment and meet Pumpy and Pepper. She'll be finished with class.

~5:30–6:30 PM: Dinner with Colleen at a restaurant nearby, to be picked with her. Drive back to cabin after dinner.

We removed Mega Cavern from Tuesday's plan because the tram is closed for the year and the zipline isn't open Tuesdays. Short walks and seated breaks are built into the day; no forced long hikes or standing tours. Times and travel estimates are adjustable.
